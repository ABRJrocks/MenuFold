# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users whose menu bar has become crowded with utilities, status indicators, and background apps. They want a calmer desktop without losing quick access to the tools they rely on throughout the day.

## Product Purpose

Create a native macOS menu-bar utility that lets people hide and reveal selected menu-bar items from one compact, dependable control. It should launch at login, stay out of the way, and make a busy menu bar feel intentionally organized.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly powerful. The experience should combine the familiarity and restraint of first-party macOS software with the speed and focus of Raycast.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded settings dashboards, gratuitous glass effects, neon power-user styling, novelty interactions, and decorative UI that competes with the user’s menu-bar content. Do not imitate another menu-bar organizer’s branding or visual identity.

## Design Principles

- Make the common action immediate: showing or hiding items should take one clear interaction.
- Feel native before feeling novel: standard macOS behaviors, materials, typography, and shortcuts establish trust.
- Reveal complexity progressively: everyday controls stay compact while permissions, rules, and advanced preferences remain easy to reach.
- Preserve user agency: clearly explain permissions, never hide an item unexpectedly, and always provide an obvious recovery path.
- Reward keyboard fluency without requiring it: every important action is discoverable with the pointer and efficient from the keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, useful VoiceOver labels and hints, strong text and control contrast, sufficient target sizes, and reduced-motion preferences. Never rely on color alone to communicate status. Permission and failure states must use plain, actionable language.
